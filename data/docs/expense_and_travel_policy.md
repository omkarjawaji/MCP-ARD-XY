# Expense and Travel Policy

Employees book travel through the approved portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.

## Limits

Meals are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day domestically and 80 USD internationally. Hotel nightly caps are set per city tier by Finance.

## Approvals

Expenses over 500 USD need manager approval before purchase. Cost-center owners review monthly budget variance against the Finance ledger.
