# Incident Escalation Runbook

## Severity levels

Sev1: full outage or data loss affecting multiple customers. Sev2: major feature degraded. Sev3: minor defect with a workaround.

## Sev1 escalation

Page the on-call engineer immediately. If there is no acknowledgement within 10 minutes, page the engineering manager.
Open an incident channel, name an incident commander, and post a status update every 30 minutes until resolved.

## After the incident

A blameless postmortem is due within 5 business days. Enterprise customers affected by a Sev1 receive a written root-cause summary from Support.
