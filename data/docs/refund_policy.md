# Refund Policy

Customers on the Pro and Enterprise plans may request a full refund within 30 days of the first invoice.
Free-plan customers are not billed and are not eligible for refunds.

## Exceptions

Custom professional-services engagements (onboarding, training) are refundable only for sessions not yet delivered.
Refunds above 10,000 USD require approval from the Finance Director.

## How to request

Open a billing ticket in the support portal with category `billing-question`. Finance processes approved refunds within 5 business days.
